Privacy Policy

App Builder · Effective 31 October 2026

App Builder is an on-device, no-code tool that lets you create real Android apps. We designed it to keep your work private: your projects stay on your phone.

1. Information we collect

  • Your app projects (names, taglines, selected features, icons, generated APKs) are stored only on your device. We do not upload them to any server.
  • No account is required. We do not ask for your name, email, or phone number to use the app.
  • Backups/restores are files you create and control via your device's file picker.

2. Advertising

This app is ad-supported. We use Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and usage information to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. Where required by law (for example in the EEA/UK), we ask for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before serving personalised ads.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Generated apps

Apps you create with App Builder are ad-free and run entirely on your device. Their data (items, orders, notes, images) is stored locally in the generated app. Sharing an APK with others is your choice.

4. Permissions we use

  • Install unknown apps — only to install the APK you just built on your own request.
  • Notifications — only for reminders you explicitly set (on demand).
  • Photos/media — only to let you pick an icon or product image; the image stays on your device.
  • Internet / network state — to load ads. The builder requires a connection to open.

5. Third parties

The only third-party service used by the builder is Google AdMob. We do not sell your data.

6. Data retention & deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You can erase it at any time using Settings → Clear all apps / Erase all data, or by uninstalling the app.

7. Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

Because your projects are stored locally, their security depends on your device. We recommend keeping your device locked and up to date.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will appear here with a new effective date.
